What is Text Dungeon?

Text Dungeon is a text-adventure style RPG. Your goal is to descend deep into an underground labyrinth, defeat the evil sorcerer 'Singor', and recover the "Ring of Life" before returning to the surface.

1
Starting, Resuming & Saving

Running TextDungeon.exe brings up the title screen with the following options:

1. New Game 2. Continue

Select an option by typing the shown number or clicking it. This input method applies throughout the game.

New Game

Select 1 to proceed to the character creation screen.

Continue

Select 2, enter your character's name, and press Enter.

Saving & Quitting

Close the application window (e.g. click the X button) to quit. A save file is created automatically. Use Continue to resume from the same point.

2
Creating Your Character

The character creation screen presents a newly generated character.
Press Enter or click Confirm to start with that character.
Press r or click Reroll to generate new parameters (unlimited times).

Items shown in blue or yellow text are manually configurable parameters.

Yellow items will automatically reroll certain stats and skills when changed.
Parameter Descriptions

Alias

Nickname, title, or self-given name. No effect on abilities or skills.

Name

The character's name. No effect on abilities or skills.

Gender

Male, Female, or Unknown. No effect on abilities or skills.

Age

Set between 15 and 99. No effect on abilities or skills.

Height

Set between 100 and 200. No effect on abilities or skills.

Weight

Set between 30 and 150. No effect on abilities or skills.

Type

Natural aptitude and growth tendency. Choose from 6 types. Changing type auto-rerolls stats and skills.

Birthplace

Choose from 10 regions. Primarily affects initial stats and skills. Changing auto-rerolls some parameters.

Regardless of type chosen, there are no restrictions on what equipment or items you can use.
Type Guide

⚔ Warrior

High VIT and BTL. Combat skills improve readily. Rarely learns magic skills; low MAG; struggles to learn spells.

🗡 Thief

High LUK. Skilled in stealth, lockpicking, and other non-combat skills. Good with SneakAttack and Throwing. Can learn some spells.

✝ Cleric

Balanced type. Handles combat and magic reasonably well. Can learn many spells and acquires a variety of skills.

✨ Mage

High MAG; can learn a very large number of spells. Starts with one Spellbook. Weak in direct combat.

⚔✨ Spellblade

Combines Warrior and Mage traits. Starts with skills from both; high VIT and BTL; can use magic from the start. Growth is slow and the ceiling is lower.

👤 Citizen

Ordinary civilian. Low VIT and BTL; starts with only one skill, rarely a combat or magic one.

Birthplace Guide

Central City

National capital with high educational standards. Tends toward higher BTL.

Trade City

Prosperous port city. Tends toward higher LUK.

Mining City

Mountain mining city. Tends toward higher VIT.

Forest Town

Entrance to the great northern forest. Bonus to Stealth.

Ruins Town

Near ancient desert ruins. Said to have higher MAG.

Crossroads Town

Where all trade routes meet; mixed cultures. May receive a variety of bonuses.

Northern Port

Cold northern coast. Physically powerful; bonus to attacks.

Plains Village

Northwestern plains settlement. Bonus to movement.

Swamp Village

Southeastern wetlands settlement. Said to have high magic resistance.

Snowfield Village

Northern snowfield settlement. Resilient; resistant to hunger and disease.

Stats

❤ VIT

Physical endurance. Reaches 0 → character dies, game over. Restore with E (eat) or R (rest).

⚡ BTL

Ability to act and handle objects. Higher = easier to hit enemies; harder for enemies to hit you. Restore with rest.

🍀 LUK

Fortune. Higher LUK = good things happen more often. Affects lockpicking and trap disarm success. Restore with rest.

✨ MAG

Magical power. Higher = stronger spell effects. Recovers slowly over time. Not displayed in-game.

Each stat has a maximum value that can be increased through level-ups and other means.
Death permanently deletes your save file. A morgue file (CharName_morgue.txt) is written to the "save" folder when you die.
Unique Ability / Skill

A special ability or skill unique to the character, granted once at creation. It can never be acquired or lost during the game.

Skill Guide

Skills are useful abilities and knowledge for adventuring. Each has a level; higher level = more proficient. Gain new skills or raise levels through level-ups and events. Maximum 8 skills. Use S to view your list; select a skill to read its description.

The Main Screen

After character creation the Main Screen appears — the primary gameplay screen showing you and your surroundings. It is divided into four areas:

↖ Upper-left: Object Display

A numbered list of objects you can interact with in your current location.

↗ Upper-right: Status Display

Your alias, name, level, experience, stats (current/max), hunger, and status effects.

↙ Lower-left: Message Display

Results of your actions. If "→More" appears, click or press any key to continue.

↘ Lower-right: Mini-map

Rooms you have visited shown as blue squares. White dot = your current position.

Interacting with Objects
1: Notice Board → read it 2: Bread Ration → pick it up 3: Short Sword → pick it up 4: 3 Goblins → attack them

Selecting an object performs the default action. Non-default actions are available via commands (e.g. press A then 1 to attack the notice board).

Status Display

Status effects appear below your stats. Yellow = negative; blue = positive. Use C to view all effects if the display is full.

Creatures
ColorMeaning
YellowHostile — has not noticed you yet
RedHostile — has noticed you; will attack every turn
BlueFriendly — selecting it talks rather than attacks
"Friendly" only means the creature is not immediately trying to eat you — it is not necessarily your ally!

Friendly creatures may carry quests. Talk to them for a "Hear Request" option. Review accepted quests anytime with Q.

Most quests have a time limit. If you fail to complete within the allotted turns, the quest fails and no reward is given.

Combat Screen & Combat

Selecting a hostile creature opens the Combat Screen:

DEX VIT 1:Goblin 5 5 2:Goblin 4 6 3:Goblin 5 4

Select a creature to attack it.

Combat Basics
  • Hit chance: your BTL, enemy BTL, weapon stats, skill level (0 if lacking) + randomness.
  • Each attack you make is met with one counter-attack (faster/slower creatures may deviate).
  • Fighting unarmed: severe penalty to both attack and defense (MartialArts skill helps).
  • Press Esc at any time to return to the Main Screen.
Fighting unarmed penalizes both attack AND defense. Always equip a weapon!
Combat-Exclusive Commands

Some commands such as R (Rest) and M (Memorize Spell) are only available on the Main Screen.

If multiple creatures tie, the one with the lowest number (highest on screen) is targeted. These keys also work when selecting a target for spells or wands.

Don't spam auto-target so fast that you miss important messages or fail to notice your VIT dropping! Use L to review scrolled messages.
5
Spells

Press m to cast a spell. Select from the list of memorized spells; some require target selection before casting begins.

Spell Parameters

Level

Spell power. Also determines how much Memory Capacity is used when memorized.

Casting Time

Turns required to cast. If Casting Time ≥ 2, taking damage or losing sight of the target interrupts casting.

VIT Cost

VIT consumed on cast completion. Reaching 0 or below kills your character!

School

The spell's elemental/magical school. Having the corresponding skill reduces cost and increases effect.

Heavy armor increases casting time by (armor base AC − 3). Example: Chain Mail (base AC=5) → +2 casting time.

Press m → Enter to view the spell list with all parameters. Press ? then select a spell to read its description.

Memorizing Spells

Use M, select a Spellbook, then choose a spell to begin memorizing it.

Memorization takes many turns. If a hostile creature appears, memorization is interrupted and must be restarted from the beginning.
Memory Capacity

The total spell levels that can be memorized at once.

Example — Capacity 4: Lv.1 spell × 1 + Lv.3 spell × 1 (1+3=4) Lv.2 spell × 2 (2+2=4)

Capacity increases with level-ups and the SpellCasting skill. Current remaining capacity is shown at the top of the M screen.

Enemy Spellcasters

Creatures that cast spells can be just as dangerous as your own magic — or more so. Dealing damage to a casting creature interrupts its spell.

Items

Acquired items are stored in your pack. All items have weight and size; you cannot exceed your carry limits.

Inventory (I command)

Shows all carried items with type, quantity, weight, and size. Page with ←/→. Select an item for details.

Click column headers ("Name," "Type," "Weight," "Size") to sort. First click = descending; subsequent clicks toggle asc/desc. "Name" sorts by symbol letter.

The top shows your gold and: Weight: current/limit   Size: current/limit

LimitHow to Increase
Weight limitLevel-up, Carry skill, or magic items (e.g. Ring of Strength)
Size limitPacking skill only
Regardless of weight/size, you can only carry up to 52 different item types (a–z, A–Z).
Identifying Items

Some items (potions, scrolls, rings, etc.) start with unidentified names like "Red Potion" or "Copper Ring." Identifying an item reveals its true name and properties.

MethodDescription
Scroll of IdentifyIdentifies one item from your pack or nearby
ShopRequest identification for a fee
SpellCast an identification spell
Use / EquipSome items are identified when used or equipped
Item Types

⚔ Weapons (Dagger, Longsword, Mace, Axe, Spear, Staff)

Equip with e. Fighting unarmed penalizes both attack and defense. Hit/damage modifiers shown after identification (e.g. "Short Sword (+1,+1)"). Staves have no modifiers but boost spellcasting. Some weapons require two hands (cannot use a shield); shown as "(Two-handed)." Two-handing grants a damage bonus (not Staves). Most weapons are identified on equip (not Artifacts).

🛡 Armor (Shield, Body, Helmet, Gauntlets, Boots, Cloak)

Equip with e. AC (Armor Class) — higher is better. AC modifier shown as [+x]. Check total AC with e or C. Example: AC:10(Shield:3) = shield AC 3, other armor total AC 10. Most armor identified on equip (not Artifacts).

💍 Rings

Equip with e (one on each hand). Magical effects — not always beneficial. Rings with modifiers (e.g. "Ring of VIT (+2)") are identified on equip (except Artifacts).

📿 Amulets

Worn around the neck (one at a time). Similar to rings but rarer effects. Amulets with modifiers are identified on equip (except Artifacts). Modifiers on rings and amulets never change.

⚠ Cursed Equipment

Weapons, armor, rings, or amulets can be cursed. Cursed items usually have negative effects and cannot be unequipped until the curse is lifted. Yellow = possibly cursed; Pink = confirmed cursed. Remove with a Scroll of Remove Curse or other magic.

☆ Artifacts

Rare legendary equipment prefixed with ☆ or ★. Unique names and powerful magic (not always positive). Not identified by equipping — use a Scroll of Identify.

🍞 Food

Essential for survival. Your character grows hungry over time; starvation drains VIT. Eat with E to relieve hunger and restore some VIT. Nutritional value varies. Some foods have magical effects.

🧪 Potions

Drink with q. Identified when consumed. Some potions are dangerous — identify safely when possible.

📜 Scrolls

Read with r to activate. Identified only when the effect successfully triggers. Consumed after being read.

📚 Books (Spellbooks)

Read with r to identify. Then use M to memorize spells from them. Reading an identified Spellbook with r shows descriptions of its spells.

🪄 Wands

Zap with z. Press @ to target yourself. Limited charges; runs out after several uses. Identified when it produces an effect (charge count is not revealed then). Use a Scroll of Recharging to restore charges.

💰 Gold

Currency for shops and transactions. Rare — spend wisely! You cannot sell items to shops.

🔑 Keys

Magic Keys open ordinary doors instantly (p command). Each use consumes one key. Rumors speak of special keys for special doors.

📦 Miscellaneous

Everything else — from important items to worthless trinkets.

7
Objects

The dungeon contains many interactable objects beyond items and creatures.

Notice Boards

Select to read the message. Messages may be meaningless or false — beware. Multiple variants exist (notice boards, bulletin boards, etc.).

Chests

Containers holding items. Almost always trapped.

  1. Select chest → attempt to identify the trap (e.g. "Wooden Box (Arrow?)")
  2. Select again → the trap to disarm is shown
  3. Select the correct trap → attempt to disarm it
Selecting the wrong trap triggers the actual trap. Even the correct choice can fail the disarm roll and trigger it.

After disarming (regardless of outcome), the chest can be freely opened. You can also store items in chests (press d in the chest item list).

Items in chests won't be picked up or eaten by creatures, but some creatures may open a chest and take items while you aren't watching.
Room Traps

Trapped rooms display a hint like "You sense something strange about the walls." The trap doesn't appear in the object list until discovered. Any action by you or a creature may trigger it.

Use b (Vigilance) to search for traps (chance based on LUK and Unlock skill). Once found, select the trap to disarm it with certainty (may take several turns).

Monuments / Statues

Stone structures. Interacting triggers a magical effect (may be harmful). Some can only be used once. First contact shows an unidentified name; touching identifies it.

In named special rooms, greet the occupant before examining objects — examining without greeting may anger them!

Commands

Most actions are performed by pressing a single letter key. Commands can also be executed from the Command List or Command Palette.

Command List (c)

Opens a list on the right side. Navigate with ↑/↓, execute with Enter. Switch pages with →/← (page 1: lowercase; page 2: uppercase/special). Click items to execute.

Command Palette (P)

Icon buttons. Toggle with P. Provides: a / e / d / i / I / b / v / m / C / R.

Macros

A macro lets you execute multiple commands with a single key press. Macros can be assigned to alphabetic keys (a–z / A–Z) or F1–F12.

Example — casting "Magic Missiles": Normal: m → a (2 keystrokes) With macro: F1 (1 keystroke)
Editing macro.txt

Edit macro.txt in the save folder of the game's install directory. Macros are shared across all characters and loaded on game start.

# macro.txt example a:ma # 'a' key → cast the spell with symbol 'a' F1:Da!b # F1 key → drop all of items a and b
Special KeyHow to Write
Enter~ (tilde) or {ENTER}
Esc{ESC}
← key{LEFT} (or h)
↑ key{UP} (or k)
↓ key{DOWN} (or j)
→ key{RIGHT} (or l)
Macros cannot contain other macros (F1–F12 keys) — this prevents infinite loops. During macro execution, only one screen of messages is shown; all messages are recorded in the log (L to review).
The ! Command & Interrupt Options

! is a macro-only command that interrupts the macro when the configured conditions are met.

-----Interrupt Options----- (1) '!' interrupts when enemies are nearby: No (2) '!' interrupts when VIT <= 5: No '!' interrupts when both (1) and (2): Yes -----Always Interrupt Options----- (3) Always interrupt when enemies are nearby: No (4) Always interrupt when VIT <= 5: No Always interrupt when both (3) and (4): No Always interrupt when paralyzed: Yes

Change ": Yes" to anything else to disable that condition. Change the number in "VIT <= x" to adjust the threshold. "Always" options trigger regardless of whether ! appears in the macro.

Registering Macros In-Game (= command)

Press = in the Main Screen or Combat Screen, select a key (F1–F12), enter the keystrokes, then press F1 to confirm. The macro is registered and macro.txt is updated automatically.

Press = → = again to immediately reload macro.txt and apply any file edits.

To reset macros to default: delete macro.txt from the "save" folder and restart the game. A default macro.txt will be regenerated.
